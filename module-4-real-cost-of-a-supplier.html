How does the model calculate the costs?

All calculations use an annual basis. The model intentionally simplifies a real sourcing analysis so you can see how each cost layer changes the decision.

  • Annual purchase cost = annual demand × quoted unit price.
  • Total landed cost = purchase cost + freight + tariffs + inbound handling.
  • Defect cost = annual demand × defect rate × estimated cost per defective unit.
  • Inventory carrying cost = purchase cost × lead-time fraction of a year × annual carrying rate.
  • Expected disruption cost = annual disruption probability × estimated financial impact.
  • TCO = TLC + defect cost + inventory carrying cost + expediting + supplier management + expected disruption cost − support benefits.

Instructor-created fictional scenario for educational use. Results are estimates, not promises about supplier performance.
