The sourcing lesson

A supplier is not simply “cheap” or “expensive.” The preferred option is conditional on volume, order fit, logistics, quality, risk, and what the business values most.

How does the activity calculate the costs?

All figures use a simplified first-year model. Open the list below if you want to inspect the mechanics.

  • Committed units = the greater of the supplier minimum or demand rounded up to the supplier’s required order multiple.
  • Purchase cost = committed units × quoted unit price.
  • TLC = purchase cost + freight + tariffs + inbound handling.
  • Quality cost = expected demand × defect rate × cost per defective unit.
  • Pipeline carrying cost = expected demand × unit price × lead-time fraction of a year × carrying rate.
  • Excess-unit carrying cost = units above expected demand × unit price × carrying rate.
  • Expected disruption cost = disruption probability × estimated interruption impact.
  • TCO = TLC + quality + pipeline inventory + excess inventory + expediting + supplier management + expected disruption − support benefits.
